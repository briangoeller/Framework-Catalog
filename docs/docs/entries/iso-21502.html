ISO 21502

framework · project management · regulatory-standard

International standard providing guidance on project management practices, replacing ISO 21500:2012 as the practice-focused standard.

ISO 21502:2020 (Project, programme and portfolio management — Guidance on project management) is the practice-focused successor to ISO 21500:2012, issued by ISO/TC 258 as part of a broader restructuring of the 21500-series into context standards (the new ISO 21500:2021 on concepts) and practice standards. ISO 21502 retains the non-prescriptive, organization-agnostic approach of its predecessor while updating the structure to reflect integrated predictive, hybrid, and adaptive lifecycle guidance — a structural change paralleling PMBOK Guide Seventh Edition's 2021 shift. The standard organizes guidance around prerequisites for formalized project management, integrated project management practices, and management practices for projects (around 17 practice areas). ISO 21502 is voluntary and has no associated certification, but is referenced in some procurement frameworks and serves as a vocabulary and structural baseline for organizations developing tailored methodologies.

Core components

  • Prerequisites for formalized project management (organizational considerations, project governance)
  • Integrated project management practices (lifecycle phases, sponsoring, directing, initiating, controlling, closing)
  • Management practices for projects (planning, benefit management, scope, resource, schedule, cost, risk, issue, change, quality, stakeholder, communication, organisational change, reporting, information and documentation, procurement, lessons learned)
  • Guidance on tailoring for predictive, hybrid, and adaptive approaches
  • Companion ISO 21500:2021 (concepts) and ISO 21503 (programme management), ISO 21504 (portfolio management)

Primary use case

International voluntary standard for project management practice guidance; input to organizational and national framework development, particularly in non-PMI / non-AXELOS jurisdictions; vocabulary and structural baseline for tailored methodologies; translation reference between PMBOK, PRINCE2, IPMA, and other national frameworks; academic reference in international project management curricula and research.

Common criticisms

  • ISO 21502 inherits the ISO 21500 critique that voluntary, non-certified standards struggle to gain practical traction relative to PMBOK and PRINCE2 with their large certified-practitioner ecosystems and embedded commercial-training markets
  • the standard's non-prescriptive guidance approach, while methodologically defensible, leaves practitioners without the implementable specificity they need, motivating fall-back to the national frameworks ISO 21502 was meant to abstract above
  • the integrated predictive-hybrid-adaptive structure parallels PMBOK Guide Seventh Edition's 2021 shift but does not provide as much tailoring guidance as PMBOK does
  • empirical evidence of adoption is sparse and dominated by anecdotal practitioner reports
  • cost-benefit of organizational compliance with ISO 21502 vs direct use of PMBOK or PRINCE2 has not been independently established
  • the 2020 publication during the pandemic limited practitioner-community formation
  • interaction with the broader 21500-series companion standards introduces complexity that undermines the simplification rationale.
