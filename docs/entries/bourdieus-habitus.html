Bourdieu's Habitus

Also known as: Field, Capital

framework · sociology · organizing-schema

Practice as the product of habitus, field, and forms of capital — economic, cultural, social.

Pierre Bourdieu's framework treats social practice as the product of three interlocking concepts: habitus (durable embodied dispositions acquired through socialization), field (a structured social space with its own logic and stakes), and capital (resources of various forms — economic, cultural, social, symbolic — that confer position and power within fields). Practice emerges from the interaction of habitus and field, with capital determining one's effective position. The framework's power lies in dissolving the dichotomy between structural determinism and individual agency by treating dispositions as both structured by past experience and structuring of future action.

Common criticisms
