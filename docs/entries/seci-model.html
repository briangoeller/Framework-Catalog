SECI Model

Also known as: Knowledge Spiral

framework · knowledge management · organizing-schema

Nonaka and Takeuchi: socialization, externalization, combination, internalization.

Ikujiro Nonaka and Hirotaka Takeuchi's model describes knowledge creation as a continuous spiral through four conversion modes between tacit and explicit knowledge: Socialization (tacit to tacit through shared experience), Externalization (tacit to explicit through articulation), Combination (explicit to explicit through systematic combination), and Internalization (explicit to tacit through embodied practice). The framework grew from study of Japanese knowledge-creating companies and has become foundational in knowledge management theory, though it has been criticized for ambiguity about what counts as tacit knowledge and for cultural specificity to its Japanese origin context.

Common criticisms

  • Ambiguity in operationalizing tacit knowledge
  • cultural specificity to Japanese organizational context
  • conceptual conflation of Polanyi's original tacit knowledge concept with practitioner-friendly simplification
  • difficulty empirically validating the four-mode structure.
